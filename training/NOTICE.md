Training code snapshot for ArrangementBuddy (P8-C).

Sources (MIT):
- ACE-Step: https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step
- Side-Step / ACE-Step Training V2: https://github.com/koda-dernet/Side-Step
  (snapshot used by `acestep/training` + `acestep/training_v2`)

Bundled minimal subset:
- acestep/__init__.py
- acestep/models, acestep/training, acestep/training_v2 (code only; checkpoints are downloaded separately from HuggingFace)
- pyshim/sitecustomize.py (torchaudio.load -> soundfile compatibility shim, needed on Windows/limited codecs)
- training/runner.py, training/requirements.txt

This bundle contains no model weights and no audio data. Model weights come from the public
HuggingFace repo ACE-Step/Ace-Step1.5 (MIT); training datasets are self-generated by ACE-Step
and published separately under the repo's training-data-v1 release.
